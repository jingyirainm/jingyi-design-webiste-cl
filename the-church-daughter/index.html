INTRODUCTION

Worked as Production Designer and Costume Designer for a religion-themed independent short film, developing the visual identity across both set and costume design. The project involved complex environments including church stage installations, domestic interiors, and character costume styling, with a focus on creating a poetic and symbolic atmosphere that reflected themes of faith, ritual, and emotional intimacy.
