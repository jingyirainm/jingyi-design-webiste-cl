INTRODUCTION

A playable dream: a video game and live audiovisual installation where players drift through chapters of a dreamscape, guided by poetry, sound and gesture. I led the project end to end, including concept, game and interaction design, visual direction and the final installation. It was presented at Accent Sisters in New York.
