Cave Song- Cultural Heritage Preservance App


Cave Song



Designed a digital cultural heritage platform dedicated to preserving and promoting China's Mogao Caves, including educational resources, digital exhibitions, and cultural products to make historical heritage more accessible to new generations.
Part of the Project from Columbia Digital Storytelling Lab.

INTRODUCTION

This website explores the Mogao Caves through an interactive digital experience that blends cultural heritage, education, and immersive storytelling. By combining visual archives, Buddhist philosophy, historical narratives, and game-inspired interactions, the platform reimagines how audiences engage with ancient Dunhuang art in the contemporary digital age.

Users are invited to virtually navigate cave spaces, uncover hidden stories, restore fragmented murals, and interact with symbolic elements inspired by Buddhist aesthetics and Silk Road history. Through sound, image, and exploration, the project transforms cultural learning into an intuitive and emotionally engaging journey.

Rather than functioning solely as an archive, the website acts as a living digital environment — one that bridges preservation, participation, and contemporary media design.
