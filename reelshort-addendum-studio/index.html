Overview & Context

When film production changes, paperwork follows.

Film production rarely goes exactly as planned. Overtime, schedule changes and last-minute adjustments can all affect the original contract terms and final payments. On top of that, labor rules and documentation requirements vary by location and employment type.

Every adjustment needs to be calculated, documented and approved before payment can move forward. It is an essential part of production, and a time-consuming one.

Every contractor on a ReelShort shoot moves through the same paperwork pipeline, tracked row by row in a crew batch sheet. Seven of the steps are "send and collect". The addendum is different: it turns negotiated terms and the production's time record into a pay calculation.

The Problem

Too much manual work, too much room for error.

The existing process relied heavily on spreadsheets and manually drafted addendums. For each contract adjustment, the team had to cross-check time records, recalculate payments, prepare documents and send them through review.

Even small mistakes could mean going back to fix the paperwork, slowing down approvals and potentially delaying payments.

The Opportunity & Winning the Buy-in

What if we could turn hours of paperwork into a few clicks?

As ReelShort began exploring AI and internal tools to improve operational efficiency, I saw an opportunity in a workflow I dealt with regularly.

Instead of drafting every addendum from scratch, I proposed building a tool that could bring production data, payment calculations, applicable rules and document generation into one place. The goal was simple: less manual work, fewer errors and faster approvals.

I presented my idea and an early demo, and got approval to move forward with this self-initiated project. I took the lead on product design, turning an idea to improve my own workflow into a tool that could also save my teammates time and help the company better organize and keep track of important documents.

Design Decisions

Drawing on my firsthand experience with the workflow, and on the need for accuracy in payroll and legal documents, I made three key design decisions to guide the product.

01 · Simplify Complexity

From four fragmented steps to one streamlined workflow.

Previously, creating an addendum required four separate stages: calculating overtime from time records, applying payroll rules and reconciling spreadsheets, drafting legal documents, and coordinating approvals.

I redesigned this fragmented process into a single workflow. Users can import time records and payroll spreadsheets, review pay components, automatically calculate adjustments, generate standardized legal clauses, and batch-export PDFs, all within one interface.

Design impactConsolidated a four-stage process into one streamlined experience, reducing repetitive work and cognitive load.

02 · Accuracy by Design

Embed compliance into the workflow, not as an afterthought.

Payroll calculations vary by location and can involve complex overtime tiers, meal penalties and contractual requirements.

I integrated location-specific calculation rules and relevant legal language for California and Texas production workflows. Instead of applying formulas by hand, users enter overtime hours and let the system calculate the applicable adjustments, with the breakdown clearly displayed for verification.

Design impactReduced reliance on manual calculations and made complex payroll adjustments easier to understand, verify and document.

03 · Human-Centered Automation

Automate repetitive tasks without removing human control.

Legal and financial documents require human judgment, even when much of the preparation can be automated.

I designed an editable workflow where users can review and modify generated information before finalizing documents. The system flags discrepancies between entered values and calculated amounts, helping users catch potential errors while keeping control over critical decisions.

Design impactBalanced automation with human oversight, making document generation faster, more transparent and easier to review.

Reflection

Start with the real problem

Working with addendums myself gave me a clear view of where the process was slow and frustrating. But I learned that the goal was not just to automate paperwork. It was to rethink the workflow and make the whole process easier for everyone using it.

Automation isn't always the answer

When working with payroll and legal documents, getting things right matters more than making everything automatic. I learned to balance automation with clear calculations, error warnings and the flexibility for users to review and make changes.

Keep learning after launch

Seeing the tool used in real productions was especially rewarding. It reminded me that shipping a product is just the beginning. As more people use it, I want to keep learning from their feedback and improve how it fits into their daily work.
