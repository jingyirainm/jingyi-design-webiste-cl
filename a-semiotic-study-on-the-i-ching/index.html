“A Semiotic Study on the I Ching” (2024)
Interactive Installation/Generative Art/Video Installation

INTRODUCTION

Being an enthusiast of classical Chinese culture, I am fascinated by the philosophy of the I Ching. By generating eight basic elements through the combination of two yin and yang symbols, The I Ching forms sixty-four hexagrams which provides a tool for understanding the world. However, from a literary perspective, the I Ching is a genre of writing that utilizes symbols, guided by visual logic that is not perceived as sacred, inevitable, or unchangeable. It is not a stone tablet left by gods or extraterrestrials but a creation of humans. It possesses a recognizable logic, a comprehensible, and even replicable creative process. To me, it is an open framework. 

In my attempt to create this project, I have rewritten and expanded upon the I Ching within this framework and aim to make its original/contemporary meaning accessible to more people through sound visualizations by coding language,p5js. 

In this project,I take the natural sound tracks represented by the I Ching Bagua to visualize them as images and vibrating lines in coding language P5js.






Procedures：

Step 1: 
Record the sounds of eight natural elements in nature, each corresponding to one of the eight trigrams in the I Ching.
Step 2: 
Import the recorded sounds into p5.js.
Step 3: 
Use p5.js to visualize the eight natural sounds as generative images that reflect the characteristics of each natural element. All visuals are generated using algorithmic data.
Step 4: 
Create an individual audio track for each natural sound.
Step 5: 
Pair the visualized sound images in every possible combination (8 × 8 = 64) to depict specific natural scenes.
