I bring structure to creativity.

Hi, I’m Jingyi , a product designer based in New York. At ReelShort, I design B2B SaaS tools that power production workflows for short dramas reaching 100M+ views. With a background in art and technology from Columbia University, I prototype in code and bring designs through to launch. Download CV

Off-work, I’m a visual artist/photographer (), a ceramics enthusiast (), and a meditation practitioner (). I’m learning to notice the delicate things — bringing that same attention to detail and care into my design work.

A New York–based studio at the intersection of art, technology, AI and visual culture.
