About the project
A semiotic study of the I Ching
“It is not a stone tablet left by gods, but a creation of humans. To me, it is an open framework.”

The I Ching generates eight basic elements from two symbols, yin and yang, and from them sixty-four hexagrams: a tool for understanding the world. Read as literature, it is a genre of writing guided by visual logic, with a recognizable, comprehensible, even replicable creative process.

Yi rewrites and expands the I Ching inside that framework, using sound visualization in p5.js to make its original and contemporary meanings accessible to more people.

Method
1

Record the sounds of eight natural elements, one for each trigram.

2

Import the recordings into p5.js.

3

Visualize each sound as a generative image that reflects its element. Every visual is generated from algorithmic data.

4

Create an individual audio track for each natural sound.

5

Pair the images in every combination, 8 × 8 = 64, to depict specific natural scenes.

Concept, sound, p5.js visuals and design: Jingyi Zhang, Rainmelon Studio. Based on the 2024 installation. In this prototype the field recordings are stood in for by synthesized audio.
