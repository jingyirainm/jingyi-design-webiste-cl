Why Knotboard

Your best idea is already saved. Somewhere.

A poster on Instagram. A thought in Notes. A plan in your calendar. A file called final_v3. Every tool works on its own. The problem lives in between: each handoff drops the reason the idea mattered.

What research found

It isn’t just Maya. Across three creative roles (design students, content creators and design teams), the same four breaks kept showing up between tools.

She doesn’t need another bookmark. She needs a path from idea to action.

Behind the design

The strongest choices protect how creative work really behaves.

Six product decisions held the system together, each one a trade-off against what a typical productivity tool would do.

01

Capture before classification

A heavy form makes people postpone saving. Record first, enrich when the value is clearer.

02

Keep tasks unscheduled

Exploration doesn’t always have a date. The Backlog gives it a home without false urgency.

03

Separate completion from output

Some work makes a file; some makes knowledge or a decision. Requiring a deliverable distorts both.

04

Use one source of truth

Duplicate task records create conflicts and weaken trust. Views differ; the record doesn’t.

05

Pair metrics with reflection

Time and points show activity. A written review explains why it mattered and what’s next.

06

Keep AI contextual

Suggestions are useful when they can reference the object, page and project already in view.

Reflection

Creative productivity isn’t doing more. It’s keeping meaning across time.

Knotboard could have become a bigger to-do list. The research pushed it elsewhere: the reference that sparked the work, the decision that shaped the plan, the effort it took and the lesson afterward all belong to the same story. Designing it stretched me across strategy, information architecture, interaction, brand and front end, and taught me that AI is only valuable when it uses context responsibly and keeps the user’s judgment visible.

Context over capture

Information architecture made the system coherent: every object remembers where it came from.

Calm over gamified

A visual language that lets a dense workspace feel quiet, with points that motivate without judging.

Assist, never override

AI drafts; people decide. Approval is part of the interaction, not an afterthought.
