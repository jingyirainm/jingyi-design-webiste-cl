INTRODUCTION
 
Through my photography series The Oasis, I document the growth and breath of plants, hot springs, glaciers, mist, land, and dew. I aim to awaken our sensitivity to the natural world and invite viewers into my sensory world — a non-anthropocentric symbiotic space where we can rethink the relationships between natural entities and ecosystems.
