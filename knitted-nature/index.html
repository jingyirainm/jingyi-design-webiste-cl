INTRODUCTION

Knitted Nature is a fashion photography project created in collaboration with designer Yingdi Xiong. Centered around the relationship between textiles and plants, the project explores the idea of “co-weaving” — where knitted fabrics, organic forms, and the natural environment become intertwined through texture, color, and movement.
