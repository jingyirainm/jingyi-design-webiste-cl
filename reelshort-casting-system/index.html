Reelshort Casting System




Redesigned and launched an internal casting platform for the leading global short- drama company, featuring a talent database and project management dashboard to support faster production decisions across high-volume TV productions.

ROLE

PRODUCT DESIGNER
BRAND DESIGNER
SCOPE

Product  Design

 Overview  

ReelShort produces a high volume of short-form dramas every year, which means casting teams constantly need to find, evaluate, and coordinate with a large pool of actors.

The existing Casting Hub helped centralize this process, but finding the right talent still required navigating dense profiles, incomplete information, multiple platforms, and repetitive communication.

I redesigned the platform around one goal: Make casting decisions faster with less manual work.


 




  The Problem  

Imagine you are a producer/casting director casting a new short drama.You need to search for actors across IMDb, Instagram, agencies, and internal records.  Then you compare photos, check availability and location, organize contact information, reach out, and track responses.

A lot of time is spent simply collecting information before a casting decision can even happen.The opportunity was to turn the Casting Hub from a talent database into a more useful decision-making tool.






  User Research  

Before redesigning the platform, I wanted to understand what casting professionals actually pay attention to. I did research with five Producers and Casting Directors working across short-form entertainment and fashion about how they find talent, compare candidates, and make final decisions.

I spoke with five Producers and Casting Directors working across short-form entertainment and fashion. Four needs came up repeatedly: Visual Fit, Complete Information, Chemistry, Agency Network. These insights became the foundation of the redesign.





  Key Design Decisions  

(All data shown is fictional demo data for demonstration reason. Real user data is strictly protected and will not be displayed.)

  01 · Make Talent Card More Visual  

Multiple photos can be viewed directly from the card, while the information hierarchy prioritizes the details needed for quick decisions. Casting teams can screen talent from the grid and open fewer profiles along the way.





   

  

 02 · Surface Information Completeness   

I identified 12 key data points commonly needed during casting and introduced a Profile Completeness indicator. Casting teams can quickly see which profiles are ready for consideration and which are missing critical information.





  03 · Rebuild Agency Library Page  

I redesigned the Agency Library to connect agencies directly with the actors they represent.  Casting teams can move between an agency, its contacts, and its talent pool without treating them as separate databases.







  04 · Change the Filter’s Information Architecture  

The original filter offered comprehensive options, but the structure was cluttered and single-select interactions made complex searches inefficient.
I reorganized the hierarchy, merged overlapping criteria, removed unnecessary options, and introduced multi-select filtering.A cleaner, more flexible filtering experience helps casting teams narrow down talent faster.







  05 · VFX Information UI  System’s  Update  

The VFX grading standard is the reference the team uses to grade every project's visual effects, from D (simple shot) to S+ (top-tier difficulty).  
I turned the old full-width table into a collapsible card with colour-coded grade chips, a lighter table and a short pricing footnote. The project list now sits near the top of the VFX page, and the full standards are still one click away.







 Outcome  

Casting looks like picking faces, but most of it is keeping track of information. One role can mean hundreds of actors, each with photos, measurements, an agency, consent limits and a schedule. Before the ReelShort Casting Hub, all of that lived in spreadsheets, inboxes and people's memory. I designed the hub to bring it into one place.
The hub now holds 600+ actors, 130+ agencies and 60+ projects, and it has become part of a larger internal system. It didn't replace casting judgment. It put everything the team needs in front of them.
