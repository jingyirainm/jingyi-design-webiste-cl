INTRODUCTION

“Mindspace” visualizes the subconscious as a surreal architectural landscape shaped by memory, emotion, and imagination.
Through minimalist white spaces, floating objects, and symbolic figures, the project transforms inner psychological states into dreamlike cinematic environments that exist between reality and hallucination.
